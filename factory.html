Factory FAQ

Can I audit the factory remotely?
Yes. We provide live video walkthroughs and photo documentation. A formal BV/SGS audit report (MIC-ASI261527) is available for review.
What certifications do you hold?
We are a BV-audited Gold Member on Made-in-China. Our fabric suppliers provide OEKO-TEX and BSCI documentation on request.
How do you handle defective goods?
Defective pieces found during final inspection are remade. If defects reach your shipment, we cover replacement costs on the next order. We stand behind every piece.
