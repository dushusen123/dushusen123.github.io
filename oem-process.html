5 Steps to Your Custom Collection

Every order follows the same proven workflow. You know exactly where your order is at every stage.

1

Design & Tech Pack

Send sketches, reference samples or existing designs. Our team creates tech packs with specs, measurements and BOM.

2

Sample (5–7 days)

We produce a physical sample for your approval. Revisions included until you sign off.

3

Print & Embroider

Screen print, DTG, 3D puff, embroidery, chenille — applied after cutting, before sewing.

4

Bulk Production

15–30 days depending on quantity. We send production photos and weekly updates.

5

QC & Pack

5-stage inspection. AQL standard checking. Individual poly-bag, carton packing.

Process FAQ

What is a tech pack and do I need one?
A tech pack is the blueprint of your garment: measurements, fabric specs, artwork placement and construction details. If you don't have one, we can build it from your sketch or reference sample.
How long does sample approval take?
Initial samples take 5–7 working days. Revision samples take 3–5 days. We ship samples internationally (DHL/FedEx, 3–5 days).
Can I visit the factory before ordering?
Absolutely. We welcome factory tours in Zhongshan, Guangdong. Contact us to schedule. We also provide live video tours if travel isn't possible.
What payment terms do you accept?
T/T (bank transfer): 30% deposit, 70% before shipment. FOB Zhongshan/Shenzhen terms.
