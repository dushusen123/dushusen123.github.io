Product FAQ

Can I customize the fabric and colors?
Yes. We work with a wide range of cotton, polyester blends, fleece and technical fabrics. Pantone matching available for bulk orders.
Do you provide tech pack support?
Yes. Send us sketches, reference samples or tech packs and our in-house design team will refine them for production.
What is the typical FOB price range?
Basic t-shirts start at $7.5/pc, detailed custom pieces range up to $12/pc. Exact quote depends on fabric weight, printing method and order quantity.
