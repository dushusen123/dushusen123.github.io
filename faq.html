Frequently Asked Questions

Everything buyers ask before placing an order. Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

Ordering & MOQ

What is your minimum order quantity?
Our standard MOQ is 100 pieces per design. For basic t-shirt styles with available stock fabrics, we may support smaller trial orders. Contact us with your design and quantity for an exact number.
Can I mix colors or sizes in one order?
Yes. Within the 100-piece MOQ, you can split across sizes (S-XXL typically) and up to 3 colors. Larger color splits require slightly higher MOQ per color.
Do I need to order in bulk from day one?
No — that's why our MOQ starts at 100. We designed it for new brands testing the market. Once you prove demand, scaling to 500+ or thousands is straightforward.

Sampling

How long does a sample take?
5–7 working days after we receive your design files, tech pack, or reference sample. Sample revisions take 3–5 days. International shipping via DHL/FedEx adds 3–5 days.
Is the sample fee refundable?
Sample fees are generally credited against your bulk order once production begins. Exact terms depend on complexity — ask when you send your design.
What do I need to provide for a sample?
Either: (1) a tech pack with measurements and artwork, (2) a reference sample you already own, or (3) clear sketches/photos with descriptions. We build the tech pack for you from any starting point.

Production & Lead Time

How long is bulk production?
Standard lead time is 15–30 days after sample approval, depending on quantity and complexity. Screen print orders are faster; detailed embroidery or garment dyeing adds time.
Can you handle rush orders?
Sometimes, depending on our current production schedule. Contact us with your deadline — if we can fit it, we will.

Pricing & Payment

What payment methods do you accept?
T/T (bank wire transfer): 30% deposit to start production, 70% balance before shipment. We work on FOB Zhongshan/Shenzhen terms.
How is pricing calculated?
FOB price = fabric + manufacturing + printing/embroidery + packaging + margin. It depends on fabric weight, print method, quantity and size range. Basic t-shirts start at $7.5/pc; detailed custom pieces range to $12/pc.
Are there hidden costs?
No. The quote we give includes fabric, production, your chosen decoration, standard poly-bag packing and export cartons. If you need custom labels, hang tags or special packaging, we quote that upfront as a separate line.

Quality & Shipping

How do you control quality?
5 inspection stages: fabric incoming, cutting, sewing, processing (print/embroidery), and final inspection at AQL 2.5 standard. You receive production photos during bulk and a final inspection report before shipment.
What if I receive defective goods?
Report it with photos within 7 days of receipt. We either credit the defective pieces on your next order or produce replacements. Defective rates are very low due to our 5-stage QC.
Do you ship to my country?
We work FOB China. You can use your own freight forwarder, or we can recommend reliable forwarders for door-to-door delivery to the US, UK, EU and Australia.

Our Factory

Where is your factory located?
Zhongshan, Guangdong, China — about 1 hour from Guangzhou and Shenzhen. This puts us in the heart of the Pearl River Delta apparel cluster.
Can I visit or audit the factory?
Yes. We welcome in-person visits and provide live video tours. Our BV audit report (MIC-ASI261527) is available for review before you decide.
How fast will you respond?
Average response time is under 3 hours during business days (GMT+8). WhatsApp is the fastest channel.
