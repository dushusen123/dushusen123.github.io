Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what buyers ask us most.

What is your minimum order quantity?
Our standard MOQ is 100 pieces per design. For basic styles and stock fabrics, we can discuss smaller trial orders. Contact us with your specs.
How long is the sample lead time?
Samples take 5–7 working days after we receive your design files or reference sample. Bulk production is 15–30 days depending on order volume.
What printing and embroidery techniques can you do?
Screen printing, DTG, puff print, 3D embroidery, chenille, heat transfer, and garment dyeing. See our OEM process page for details.
