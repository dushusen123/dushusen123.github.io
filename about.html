About Dadu Du

A family-run apparel factory that has grown with brands — not just for them.

Our Story

Dadu Du Clothing was established over 12 years ago in Zhongshan, Guangdong — one of China's oldest and most complete apparel manufacturing clusters. What began as a small sewing workshop has grown into a full-service OEM/ODM facility serving brands in North America and beyond.

Over the years we've handled thousands of foreign-trade orders across t-shirts, hoodies, functional jackets, knitwear and sportswear. We built our own R&D design team and standardized production lines so that customers get one accountable partner from first sketch to final carton — no middlemen, no outsourcing.

Today we're known for flexible MOQ (starting at 100 pieces), transparent FOB pricing, and a response time under 3 hours. Whether you're a new brand testing your first collection or an established retailer scaling up, we treat your order like our own.
